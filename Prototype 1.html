      Weights follow the research already gathered: academic pressure (66.2%) and role overload (50.4%) as the top burnout factors among Malaysian medical and health sciences students; a 2.07 odds ratio for burnout under poor sleep among Perak undergraduates; social support negatively correlated with burnout. The four baseline questions are structured on the Burnout Assessment Tool's core dimensions but written from scratch — the BAT is copyrighted and its authors state plainly that no self-report tool diagnoses burnout. CapCoach estimates load. It does not diagnose.
